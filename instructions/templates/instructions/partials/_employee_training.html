Employee's Trainings


After Periods 2, 3 and 4, every employee will complete a training. The three trainings are designed to help employees make better project selection decisions.

After completing the training after Period 4, employees will complete two additional periods without further trainings.

Each training will focus on different ways on how to improve the project selection.

Please note that once an employee took a training, they can revisit this training and its insights during every round. However, each time an employee revisits a training, they will incur a Training Revisit Cost of 100 points. To calculate an employee's Performance, this cost will be subtracted from an employee's Realized Project Value in that round.

Each training lasts a minimum of 90 seconds to ensure everyone has enough time to review the material. After 4 minutes on the training, the employee will be asked to proceed to the next period.

Once an employee has finished all periods and has responded to the questionnaire at the end of today’s study, they can enter their bank details for payment and leave.
