Employee's Performance and Compensation


Employees will receive a compensation which depends on the sum of employees’ Period Performance over all periods.

An employee’s Performance represents the final performance score of an employee for the task. Specifically, an employee’s Performance equals the selected project’s Realized Project Value less Total Reveal Costs and less Total Training Revisit Costs.

That means, an employee’s Performance in each round is given by:

Performance = Realized Project Value of selected project – Total Reveal Costs – Total Training Revisit Costs

The Performance of each of the six rounds of a period adds up to the employee’s Period Performance.

An employee’s compensation is based on the sum of the employee’s Period Performance over all periods. It will be converted into cash at a rate of 1 € per 3’000 points. This will be added to the 3.00 € participation fee.

This means that the higher the Performance in every round, the higher the compensation employees will receive for today’s study.
